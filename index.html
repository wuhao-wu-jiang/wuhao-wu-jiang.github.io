Biography

I am a William T. Tutte Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Waterloo, hosted by Florian Kerschbaum. Prior to this, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Copenhagen, supported by the Providentia project and affiliated with Basic Algorithms Research Copenhagen (BARC), hosted by Rasmus Pagh. I completed my PhD at the University of Melbourne, advised by Anthony Wirth and Olga Ohrimenko.

Upcoming: In Spring 2027, I will begin a new position as an Assistant Professor in the School of Data Science at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen.

Prospective Students / RAs / Interns

I am happy to hear from motivated and curious students interested in my research. Students with strong mathematical maturity and/or programming skills are especially welcome. Feel free to reach out by email if you are interested in working with me.

Making Privacy Scale

I design privacy-preserving algorithms for large-scale data analysis, aiming for strong utility guarantees while keeping them fast, implementable, and amenable to secure and distributed computation.

Beyond privacy, I have broad interests in algorithm design, particularly sketching algorithms, graph algorithms, and learning-augmented algorithms.
