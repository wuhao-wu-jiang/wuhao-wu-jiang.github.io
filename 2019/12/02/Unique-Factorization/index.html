Unique Factorization

02 December 2019Hao Wu~2 min read

A prime number is a positive integer greater than 1 whose only divisors are 1 and itself.

Theorem (Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic). For every n∈N+n \in \mathbb{N}^+n∈N+ with n>1n > 1n>1, nnn has a unique prime factorization. That is, we can write

n=p1p2⋯pk, n = p_1 p_2 \cdots p_k, n=p1​p2​⋯pk​,

p1≤p2≤⋯≤pk. p_1 \le p_2 \le \cdots \le p_k. p1​≤p2​≤⋯≤pk​.
